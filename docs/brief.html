"An evidence-guided privacy compiler that unifies heterogeneous English, Hindi, and Hinglish hospital records across disparate systems without leaking patient identities or degrading clinical utility."

1. In One Paragraph

Hospitals in Yugaantar Pradesh merge clinical records across English, Devanagari Hindi, and Hinglish spanning 4 disparate hospital silos. Conventional PII scrubbers fail catastrophically: naive redactors destroy essential medical utility (scrubbing blood pressures, drug dosages, and eponyms) while leaving patients vulnerable to cross-script transliteration traps, family-shared phones, and quasi-identifier re-identification attacks. RAKSHA solves this with an Evidence-Guided Privacy Compiler: it ingests multi-format data safely, applies a role-first clinical veto, constructs a cross-script phonetic entity graph with negative evidence constraints, enforces risk-gated k-anonymity with guaranteed true-age containment, and executes an exhaustive (N)$ sweep verifier to guarantee mathematical zero-leak enforcement.

3. How Each Component Works

  • Safe Streaming Ingestion: Resiliently handles CSV, TSV, JSON, FHIR R4 resources, and unstructured clinical text across arbitrary encodings (UTF-8, UTF-16, CP1252). Implements bounded parsing with strict depth guards (>100 levels trigger automatic quarantine) and memory-conscious streaming, eliminating decompression bombs and memory exhaustion.
  • Role-First Multilingual Detector: Solves the Meaning Trap by distinguishing clinical complaints from relative names (e.g. "C/O fever" vs "C/O Ramesh", "S/o", "W/o", "D/o") and protecting physician names ("Dr. Sharma"). A protected clinical lexicon of 250+ drugs, lab units, and eponyms vetoes false redactions, preserving vital dosages ("Dolo 650 BD") and vitals ("120/80 mmHg"). Novel or unlabelled tabular columns are classified via semantic fuzzy synonym matching and statistical value fingerprinting.
  • Cross-Script Phonetic Entity Linkage: Resolves the Language Trap ("Mohd", "Mohammad", "मोहम्मद") via Devanagari-to-Latin transliteration, matra collapsing, honorific stripping, and double-metaphone phonetic skeletons. Employs a 3-pass evidence graph: (1) deterministic matching on strong national/hospital IDs, (2) phone-based matching with corroboration guarding against family-shared phones, and (3) fragment margin scoring requiring score ≥ 0.8 and margin ≥ 0.2 over runner-up. Must-not-link constraints strictly forbid merging conflicting IDs, opposing genders, or age gaps > 5 years. Ambiguous records safely default to patient: null.
  • Risk-Gated Privacy Planner: Evaluates 569Xanonymity against external voter registries. When demographic equivalence classes fall below =5$, an automated escalation ladder widens 5-year age bands to 10-year brackets (mathematically guaranteed to contain the patient's true age) and generalises village/district locations. Offsets all admission, discharge, and observation dates synchronously per patient cluster, preserving hospital-stay duration to the exact day.
  • Security Architecture & Sweep Verifier: RAKSHA_SECRET resides strictly in memory and is never logged or exposed. Patient pseudonyms are generated via HMAC-SHA256 in Crockford Base32 (PSN-XXXXXX). Prior to export, an exhaustive (N)$ tokenized sweep verifier scans all sanitized bodies, headers, JSON keys, and filenames against removed PII, raw Aadhaar/phone regexes, canaries, and secret tokens. Any violation immediately routes the file to quarantine.

6. Known Limitations & Design Trade-Offs

  • Linkage Conservatism: When identity evidence is ambiguous (e.g. matching name and village without phone or MRN corroboration), RAKSHA intentionally assigns patient: null. We treat false identity merges in clinical research as significantly worse than unlinked fragments.
  • Quasi-Identifier Generalization: For rare patient profiles ( < 5$), age bands are broadened to 10 years (e.g. 40-49) and fine locations suppressed to district level, sacrificing minor geographic granularity to prevent voter registry re-identification.
  • Fail-Safe Quarantine Over Partial Leaks: Corrupted files, recursion depths exceeding 100, or canary-seeded documents are quarantined in their entirety rather than partially exported, ensuring zero untrusted leakage enters the research enclave.
